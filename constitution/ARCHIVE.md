# ARCHIVE

Artifact Class: Guidance Artifact

Purpose

Define the role of archives within the PING platform.

This document is not constitutional.

This document does not define truth.

This document does not define governance.

This document defines survivability requirements for continuity artifacts.

---

Section 0 — Definition

An Archive is the survivable unit of continuity.

An archive exists to preserve the ability to reconstruct truth.

An archive is not an implementation.

An archive is not a database.

An archive is not a projection.

An archive is a portable continuity container.

---

Section 1 — Archive Contents

An archive may contain:

* events
* witnesses
* lineage
* replay metadata
* knowledge artifacts
* governance artifacts
* capability artifacts
* continuity artifacts

Archives should preserve reconstructability.

Archives should not depend upon a specific runtime.

---

Section 2 — Archive Principle

Platforms are temporary.

Archives are durable.

Implementations may be replaced.

Archives must survive implementation replacement.

---

Section 3 — Archive Independence

Archives should remain portable.

Archives should remain exportable.

Archives should remain understandable without proprietary systems.

---

Section 4 — Archive Success

An archive succeeds when a future system can reconstruct continuity without trusting the original implementation.

The purpose of an archive is continuity survivability.
