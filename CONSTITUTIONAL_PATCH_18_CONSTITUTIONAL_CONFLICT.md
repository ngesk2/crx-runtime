## SECTION 0 — VULNERABILITY IDENTIFICATION

### Attack Scenario

Year 40,000.

PING-A and PING-B both descend from the same constitutional root.

Both preserve continuity.
Both preserve replay.
Both preserve governance.
Both preserve archives.
Both preserve witnesses.

Both evolve.

Eventually they disagree.

### Question

Which one is "the real PING"?

### Current Constitution

Currently:
- Forks may exist
- Forks are observable
- Continuity survives forks

But:
- Nothing says one fork can supersede another
- Nothing says one fork can invalidate another
- Nothing says constitutional legitimacy transfers

### Failure Mode

Future political actors can claim:
- "We are the legitimate continuation."

Another fork can claim the same.

The constitution currently has no answer.
