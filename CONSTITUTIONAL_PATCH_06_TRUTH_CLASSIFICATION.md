## SECTION 1 — TRUTH CATEGORIES

### Observed Truth

**Definition:**
Direct constitutional observation.

**Properties:**
- Directly witnessed
- Not claimed by actors
- Not inferred
- Raw constitutional input

**Examples:**
- RSS feed received
- Email received
- Document ingested

### Claimed Truth

**Definition:**
Assertion by actors.

**Properties:**
- Claimed by actor
- Requires verification
- May be false
- Not constitutional authority

**Examples:**
- "Alice follows Bob"
- "Article is accurate"
- "User is verified"

### Derived Truth

**Definition:**
Inference produced by replay.

**Properties:**
- Computed from events
- Replay-reconstructed
- Deterministic
- Verifiable

**Examples:**
- Current object state
- Relationship status
- Lineage chain

### Governance Truth

**Definition:**
Authorized interpretation.

**Properties:**
- Authorized by governance
- Policy-based
- May regulate access
- Not erase continuity

**Examples:**
- Constitutional amendments
- Policy decisions
- Access restrictions

## SECTION 2 — TRUTH SEPARATION LAW

### Constitutional Law

**Truth categories MUST be separated.**

**Truth categories MUST NOT be collapsed.**

### Truth Separation Requirements

Truth separation MUST:
- Distinguish Observed from Claimed
- Distinguish Claimed from Derived
- Distinguish Derived from Governance
- Prevent truth category collapse
- Enable truth verification

### Truth Verification

Truth verification MUST:
- Identify truth category
- Verify truth source
- Validate truth claims
- Distinguish truth from falsehood

## SECTION 3 — WITNESS CORRUPTION DEFENSE

### Constitutional Law

**Witnesses do NOT make Claimed Truth into Observed Truth.**

### Witness Corruption Defense

Witness corruption MUST be defended by:
- Truth category separation
- Witness verification
- Source authentication
- Claim validation

### Witness Limitations

Witnesses MUST:
- Verify event occurrence
- NOT verify event truth
- NOT validate claims
- NOT authorize truth
